The '''2026–27 season''' is the 139th season in the history of [[Blackburn Rovers F.C.|Blackburn Rovers Football Club]] and their ninth consecutive season in the [[EFL Championship|Championship]]. In addition to the [[2025–26 EFL Championship|domestic league]], the club would also participate in the [[2025–26 FA Cup|FA Cup]], and the [[2025–26 EFL Cup|EFL Cup]].

== Season summary ==
===May===
On 19 May 2026, Rovers announced their retained list. The club confirmed they activated the one-year options in the contract of [[Kristi Montgomery]]. The club made a contract offer to [[Nathan Dlamini]], while [[Igor Tyjon]], Valentin Joseph and Bruce Leeming all had their scholarship agreements extended into a third year.<ref name='RetainList'>{{cite news |title=Retain List 2026 |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/19/retain-list-2026/ |access-date=19 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=19 May 2026}}</ref> 

The club confirmed that [[Ryan Hedges]], [[Sondre Tronstad]] and [[Nathan Redmond]] would all leave [[Ewood Park]] when their contracts expire at the end of June. Under-21s players [[Leo Duru]], [[Harley O'Grady-Macken]], [[Adam Khan (footballer)|Adam Khan]], Harrison Wood, Joe Boggan, Zack Stritch and Patrik Farkas, along with scholars Aaron Potter, Blake Wolsoncroft, Leon Kelley-Quinn and Luke Thompson, will not be retained by the club upon the expiration of their current contracts.<ref name='RetainList'/>

On 23 May 2026, Rovers announced the appointment of Steve Curwood as the club's Chief Executive Officer.<ref>{{cite news |title=Steve Curwood appointed Rovers' Chief Executive Officer |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/23/steve-curwood-appointed-rovers--chief-executive-officer/ |access-date=23 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=23 May 2026}}</ref>

===June===
On 5 June, Rovers announced the return of [[Tony Mowbray]] as head coach on a long-term contract.<ref>{{cite news |title=Tony Mowbray returns to Rovers |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/june/05/tony-mowbray-returns-to-rovers/ |access-date=5 June 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site|date=5 June 2026}}</ref>

On 11 June, Rovers announced first team coach [[Phil Jones (footballer, born 1992)|Phil Jones]] had left the club.<ref>{{cite news |title=Phil Jones leaves Rovers role |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/june/11/phil-jones-leaves-rovers-role/ |access-date=11 June 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site|date=11 June 2026}}</ref>

On 23 June, Rovers announced the appointment of [[Paul Butler (football coach)|Paul Butler]] as under-21 head coach steeping up from his role as under-18 head coach.<ref>{{cite news |title=Paul Butler appointed Under-21s Head Coach |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/june/23/paul-butler-appointed-under-21s-head-coach/ |access-date=23 June 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site|date=23 June 2026}}</ref>

On 27 June, Rovers announced the appointment of [[Mike Dodds]] as assistant head coach.<ref>{{cite news |title=Mike Dodds appointed Assistant Head Coach |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/june/27/mike-dodds-appointed-assistant-head-coach/ |access-date=27 June 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site|date=27 June 2026}}</ref>

=== July ===
On 1 July, Rovers announced new kit manufacturers [[Umbro]] in a long-term partnership.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Blackburn Rovers and Umbro UK join forces for a new era |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/july/01/blackburn-rovers-and-umbro-uk-join-forces-for-a-new-era/ |access-date=2026-07-01 |website=www.rovers.co.uk}}</ref>

On 18 July, Rovers announced the appointment of [[Gareth Stewart]] as first team goalkeeper coach.<ref>{{cite news |title=Gareth Stewart returns as Goalkeeping Coach |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/july/18/gareth-stewart-returns-as-goalkeeping-coach/ |access-date=18 July 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site|date=18 July 2026}}</ref>

On 20 July, Rovers announced that [[Damien Johnson]] had been promoted to assistant head coach & head of player development.<ref>{{cite news |title=Damien promoted to Assistant Head Coach |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/july/20/damien-promoted-to-assistant-head-coach/ |access-date=20 July 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site|date=20 July 2026}}</ref>

On 24 July, it was announced Rovers Head of Medical [[Andrew Proctor]] had left to join [[Norwich City F.C.|Norwich City]] as Head of Physiotherapy.<ref>{{cite news |title=Football and medical staffing update |url=https://www.canaries.co.uk/news/football-and-medical-staffing-update |access-date=25 July 2026 |publisher=Norwich City|date=24 July 2026}}</ref>

===August===
On 11 August, Rovers announced that Dan Stubberfield had been appointed as the new head coach of the under-18s.<ref>{{cite news |title=Dan Stubberfield appointed U18s head coach |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/august/11/dan-stubberfield-appointed-u18s-head-coach |access-date=11 August 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site|date=11 August 2026}}</ref>
