The '''2026–27 season''' is the 149th season in the history of [[Wolverhampton Wanderers F.C.|Wolverhampton Wanderers Football Club]], and their first season back being in the [[EFL Championship|Championship]] since [[2017–18 Wolverhampton Wanderers F.C. season|2017–18 season]] following relegation from the [[Premier League]] in the [[2025–26 Wolverhampton Wanderers F.C. season|preceding season]]. In addition to the [[2026–27 EFL Championship|domestic league]], the club also participate in the [[2026–27 FA Cup|FA Cup]] and the [[2026–27 EFL Cup|EFL Cup]].

== Managerial changes ==
Prior to the season starting, the club parted ways with head coach [[Rob Edwards (footballer, born 1982)|Rob Edwards]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Wolves part ways with Rob Edwards |url=https://www.wolves.co.uk/news/club/20260611-wolves-part-ways-with-rob-edwards/ |access-date=11 June 2026 |publisher=Wolverhampton Wanderers FC |date=11 June 2026}}</ref> Four days later, [[César Peixoto]] was appointed as the new head coach on a two-year contract.<ref>{{cite news |title=Cesar Peixoto appointed Wolves head coach |url=https://www.wolves.co.uk/news/mens-first-team/20260615-cesar-peixoto-appointed-wolves-head-coach/ |access-date=15 June 2026 |publisher=Wolverhampton Wanderers FC |date=15 June 2026}}</ref>
