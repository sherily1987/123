The '''2025–26 season''' was the 138th season in the history of [[Blackburn Rovers F.C.|Blackburn Rovers Football Club]] and their eighth consecutive season in the [[EFL Championship|Championship]]. In addition to the [[2025–26 EFL Championship|domestic league]], the club participated in the [[2025–26 FA Cup|FA Cup]] and the [[2025–26 EFL Cup|EFL Cup]].

== Season summary ==
===May===
On 19 May 2025, Blackburn announced their retained list. The club confirmed they activated the one-year option in the contract of [[Ryan Hedges]]. Under-21s players Harrison Wood, Zack Stritch and Adam Khan were also informed that the club had taken up the option to extend their contract by a further year. [[Tyrhys Dolan]], [[Danny Batth]], [[Andreas Weimann|Andi Weimann]], [[Jake Batty]] and [[Felix Goddard]] were offered new contracts while Lorenze Mullarkey-Matthews was offered his first professional contract.<ref name="RetainedList"/>

[[Dilan Markanday]], [[Jack Vale (footballer, born 2001)|Jack Vale]], [[Zak Gilsenan]], Patrick Gamble, [[Tom Bloxham (footballer, born 2005)|Tom Bloxham]], Jordan Eastham, Rhys Doherty, Isaac Whitehall, Jalil Saadi, Alex Baker, Adam Caddick, Paul Murphy-Worrell, Lewis Bell, Bradley Taylor, Jackson Shorrocks, Nathan Willis, Stephen Edmondson and Teddy Ramwell were not retained by the club upon the expiration of their current agreements. The club kept close contact with [[Adam Forshaw]].<ref name="RetainedList"/>

===June===
On 4 June 2025, Rovers announced u21 goalkeeper [[Felix Goddard]] had signed a new 2-year contract until 2027, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Goddard'/>

On 19 June 2025, Rovers announced [[Adam Forshaw]] had re-joined the club on a 1-year contract.<ref name='Forshaw'/>

===July===

On 1 July 2025, Rovers announced [[Jake Batty]] had signed a new contract until 2026 with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Batty'/>

On 2 July 2025, Rovers announced u21 midfielder Lorenze Mullarkey-Matthews had signed his first professional contract until 2026.<ref name='LMM'/>

On 7 July 2025, Rovers announced highly regarded defender Silver Eze had signed a two-year scholarship with the club. Rovers also announced David Odogun had joined as a scholar from [[Wolverhampton Wanderers F.C.|Wolverhampton Wanderers]].<ref name='Odogun'>{{cite news |title=Silver signs up! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/july/07/silver-signs-up-/ |access-date=7 July 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=7 July 2025}}</ref>

On 14 July 2025, it was confirmed that first team coach [[David Lowe (footballer)|David Lowe]] would be leaving the club.<ref>{{cite news |title=Valerien Ismael on the future of Blackburn Rovers coach |url=https://www.lancashiretelegraph.co.uk/sport/25311387.valerien-ismael-confirms-exit-blackburn-rovers-coach/ |access-date=16 July 2025 |publisher=Lancashire Telegraph |date=14 July 2025}}</ref>

On 23 July 2025, it was announced Rovers had appointed Nacho Sancho, who is a strength and conditioning coach. Sancho replaced Karl Hodges, Lead Sports Scientist, who left to join Derby County.<ref>{{cite news |title=New Blackburn Rovers coach spotted as Ismael adds to staff |url=https://www.lancashiretelegraph.co.uk/sport/25334692.new-blackburn-rovers-coach-spotted-ismael-adds-staff/ |access-date=23 July 2025 |work=Lancashire Telegraph |date=23 July 2025}}</ref>

===August===
On 12 August 2025, Rovers announced  highly rated young goalkeeper [[Nicholas Michalski]] has committed his long-term future to the club,  new four-year contract at Ewood Park through to June 2029, with a one-year option.<ref name='Michalski'/>

On 15 August 2025, Rovers announced [[Paul Butler (football coach)|Paul Butler]] will be the new Under-18s Lead Coach for the upcoming season, He'll be assisted by [[Ryan Kidd]] and takes over the role from Darragh Tuffy, who steps up as the new Under-21s assistant coach to support the long-serving [[Mike Sheron]], who'll continue as Under-21s Lead Coach for the coming campaign.<ref>{{cite news |title=Academy staffing changes confirmed ahead of 2025-26 |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/august/15/academy-staffing-changes-confirmed-ahead-of-2025-26/ |access-date=15 August 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=15 August 2025}}</ref>

===September===
On 3 September 2025, Rovers announced u18 defender Silver Eze had signed his 1st professional contract until 2028,<ref name='Eze'>{{cite news |title=Silver signs pro deal! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/september/03/silver-signs-pro-deal-/ |access-date=3 September 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=3 September 2025}}</ref> Rovers also announced former forward [[Jordan Rhodes]] had rejoined the club as a loan manager.<ref>{{cite news |title=Rhodes returns in new Rovers role! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/september/03/rhodes-returns-in-new-rovers-role-/ |access-date=3 September 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=3 September 2025}}</ref>

On 19 September 2025, Rovers announced under 21 head coach [[Mike Sheron]] had left the club.<ref>{{cite news |title=Mike Sheron departs |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/september/19/mike-sheron-departs-/ |access-date=19 September 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=19 September 2025}}</ref>

===October===

===November===
On 10 November 2025, Rovers announced the appointment of [[Grégory Vignal]] as the under 21 head coach, it was confirmed [[Danny Ventre]] as a set piece coach.<ref>{{cite news |title=Rovers appoint new Under-21s head coach |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/november/10/rovers-appoint-new-under-21s-head-coach/?fbclid=Iwb21leAN-zeFjbGNrA37NmmV4dG4DYWVtAjExAHNydGMGYXBwX2lkDDM1MDY4NTUzMTcyOAABHrVxfazLtm0nMISrsQEPDKMWPUUtkLSlFGXwRhJo7q1oTpH_BbgWW9fjKZjM_aem_4NkWvKP4rp2qZMFApSMYjw |access-date=10 November 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=10 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Blackburn Rovers boss drops hint over January transfer plan |url=https://www.lancashiretelegraph.co.uk/sport/25609311.blackburn-rovers-boss-drops-hint-january-transfer-plan/ |access-date=10 November 2025 |publisher=Lancashire Telegraph |date=10 November 2025}}</ref>

===December===

===January===
On 28 January 2026, Rovers announced u21 defender Max Davies had signed a contract until 2029, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Davies'>{{cite news |title=Davies pens new long-term deal! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/january/28/davies-pens-new-long-term-deal-/ |access-date=28 January 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=28 January 2026}}</ref>

===February===
On 2 February 2026, Blackburn announced [[Valérien Ismaël]] had left his position as head coach by mutual consent along with assistant head coach [[Dean Whitehead]]. [[Damien Johnson]] was placed in temporary charge.<ref name='Ismael'>{{cite news |title=Club Statement: Valérien Ismaël |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/february/02/club-statement--val-rien-isma-l/ |access-date=2 February 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=2 February 2026}}</ref><ref name='DJ'>{{cite news |title=Johnson placed in interim charge |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/february/02/johnson-placed-in-interim-charge/ |access-date=2 February 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=2 February 2026}}</ref>

On 13 February 2026, Blackburn announced the appointment of [[Michael O'Neill (footballer)|Michael O'Neill]] as head coach on a short term deal, He took charge of first team matters at the club while continuing in his role as [[Northern Ireland national football team|Northern Ireland]] manager. O'Neill brought in [[Phil Jones (footballer, born 1992)|Phil Jones]] and [[Steven Davis]] as new first team coaches. [[Damien Johnson]] continued as first team technical coach and head of player development.<ref name='ONeill'>{{cite news |title=Michael O'Neill takes charge until end of season |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/february/13/michael-o-neill-takes-charge-until-end-of-season/ |access-date=13 February 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=13 February 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Former Rover returns as part of new-look backroom staff |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/february/13/former-rover-returns-as-part-of-new-look-backroom-staff/ |access-date=13 February 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=13 February 2026}}</ref>

On 20 February 2026, Rovers announced u18 midfielder Isaac Dunn had signed his first professional contract until 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Dunn'>{{cite news |title=Isaac Dunn signs pro deal! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/february/20/isaac-dunn-signs-pro-deal-/ |access-date=20 February 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=20 February 2026}}</ref>

On 26 February 2026, Rovers announced u18 forward Harvey Higgins had signed his first professional contract until 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Higgins'>{{cite news |title=Hot-shot Harvey pens pro deal! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/february/26/hot-shot-harvey-pens-pro-deal-/ |access-date=26 February 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=26 February 2026}}</ref>

===March===
On 24 March 2026, Rovers announced u18 midfielder Frank Vare had signed his first professional contract until 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Vare'>{{cite news |title=Vare pens pro deal! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/march/23/vare-pens-pro-deal-/ |access-date=24 March 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=24 March 2026}}</ref>

===April===
On 28 April 2026, Rovers announced winger Jayden Sergeant had signed his first professional contract until 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Sergeant'>{{cite news |title=Sergeant signs pro deal! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/april/27/sergeant-signs-pro-deal-/ |access-date=28 April 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=28 April 2026}}</ref>

On 29 April 2026, Rovers announced midfielder Freddie Leatherbarrow had signed his first professional contract until 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Freddie'>{{cite news |title=Pro deal for Freddie! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/april/28/pro-deal-for-freddie-/ |access-date=29 April 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=29 April 2026}}</ref>

===May===
On 2 May 2026, [[Ryōya Morishita]] was voted Player of the Season, [[Balázs Tóth (footballer, born 1997)|Balázs Tóth]] was voted Players Player of the Season, [[Tom Atcheson]] won Young Player of the Season, [[Yūki Ōhashi]] was voted Junior Rovers Player of the Season and [[Adam Forshaw]]'s goal against [[Stoke City F.C.|Stoke City]] was voted Goal of the Season.<ref>{{cite news |title=Mori crowned Player of the Year! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/02/mori-crowned-player-of-the-year-/ |access-date=2 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=2 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Balazs scoops Players' Player accolade! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/02/balazs-scoops-players--player-accolade-/ |access-date=2 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=2 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Tom takes Young Player prize! |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/02/tom-takes-young-player-prize-/ |access-date=2 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=2 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Yuki voted Junior Rovers Player of the Year |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/02/yuki-voted-junior-rovers-player-of-the-year/ |access-date=2 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=2 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Forsh bags Goal of the Season!|url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/02/forsh-bags-goal-of-the-season/ |access-date=2 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=2 May 2026}}</ref>

On 6 May 2026, Rovers announced defender Michael Decandia had signed his first professional contract until 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Decandia'>{{cite news |title=Decandia pens pro deal |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/06/decandia-pens-pro-deal/ |access-date=6 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=6 May 2026}}</ref>

On 11 May 2026, Rovers announced defender Harvey Pates had signed his first professional contract until 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Pates'>{{cite news |title=Pates pens pro deal |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/11/Pates-pens-pro-deal/ |access-date=12 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=12 May 2026}}</ref> Rovers also announced u21 head coach [[Grégory Vignal]] had left his role.<ref>{{cite news |title=Greg leaves Rovers role |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/11/greg-leaves-rovers-role/ |access-date=12 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=12 May 2026}}</ref>

On 12 May 2026, Rovers announced head coach [[Michael O'Neill (footballer)|Michael O'Neill]] would not be extending his current agreement to become the club's permanent Head Coach beyond the conclusion of his existing period with the club.<ref>{{cite news |title=Club Statement: Michael O'Neill |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/12/club-statement--michael-o-neill/ |access-date=12 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=12 May 2026}}</ref>

On 19 May 2026, Rovers announced midfielder Tyler Mansbridge had signed his first professional contract until 2027, with the option of a further 12 months.<ref name='Mansbridge'>{{cite news |title=Tyler signs pro deal |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2026/may/18/Tyler-signs-pro-deal/ |access-date=19 May 2026 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers Official Site |date=19 May 2026}}</ref>

==Pre-season and friendlies==
On 23 April, Blackburn Rovers announced they would spend a week in Spain as part of their pre-season preparations, which would also includes two friendlies at the [[Pinatar Arena]] against [[Qatar SC|Qatar]] and [[Elche CF|Elche]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Pre-season training camp confirmed |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/april/23/pre-season-training-camp-confirmed/ |access-date=25 April 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers FC |date=23 April 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Double-header finalised for Spain trip |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/july/07/double-header-finalised-for-spain-trip/ |access-date=7 July 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers FC |date=7 July 2025}}</ref> Then on 3 June, the first two oppositions were added to the schedule, against [[Everton F.C.|Everton]] and [[Accrington Stanley F.C.|Accrington Stanley]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Everton to visit Ewood |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/june/03/everton-to-visit-ewood/ |access-date=7 June 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers FC |date=3 June 2025}}</ref> A fifth friendly was later added in July, against [[NEC Nijmegen]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Rovers go Dutch for final friendly |url=https://www.rovers.co.uk/news/2025/july/21/rovers-go-dutch-for-final-friendly/ |access-date=21 July 2025 |publisher=Blackburn Rovers FC |date=21 July 2025}}</ref>
