Findlay red card appeal dismissed

04 August 2026

The Club is disappointed to learn that our appeal against Stuart Findlay’s red card, awarded at Pittodrie on Saturday, has been dismissed.

 

Stuart will now be suspended for Sunday's Premiership match against Dundee United.

 

The club will be making no further comment.
