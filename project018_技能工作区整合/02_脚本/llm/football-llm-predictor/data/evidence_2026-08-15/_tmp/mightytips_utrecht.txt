These two clubs traditionally engage in fierce battles for European qualification, and given that they both finished in the upper echelons of the table last term, this early-season showdown presents the ideal litmus test for both teams' form at the start of the competitive year. The subsequent medical reports and current atmosphere inside the dressing room offer very little comfort to the hosts ahead of this incredibly daunting test, given that the club is currently grappling with a literal epidemic of severe injuries, leaving the likes of Miliano Jonathans, Victor Jensen, and Oualid Agougil completely sidelined with ruptured cruciate ligaments, while Alonzo Engwanda and Noah Ohio have also been added to a heavily congested treatment room.
