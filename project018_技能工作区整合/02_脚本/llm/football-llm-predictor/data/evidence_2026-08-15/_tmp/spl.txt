The '''2026–27 Saudi Pro League''', additionally named the [[Roshn]] Saudi League due to sponsorship reasons, will be the 70th season of the premier football competition in Saudi Arabia. [[Al-Nassr FC|Al-Nassr]] are the reigning champion.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-06-27 |title=Cristiano Ronaldo double fires Al Nassr to 2025-26 RSL title glory |url=https://www.spl.com.sa/en/news/1069609/www.spl.com.sa/en/news/1069609/ronaldo-double-fires-al-nassr-to-2025-26-rsl-title-glory |access-date=2026-06-27 |website=Saudi Professional League Association |language=en}}</ref>

The league started on 13 August 2026 and is scheduled to end on 29 May 2027.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://spa.gov.sa/ |title=رياضي / رابطة الدوري السعودي للمحترفين تكشف الروزنامة السنوية لموسم 2026-2027 |website=spa.gov.sa}}</ref> It will feature a break from 24 December to 7 February, as the country hosts the [[2027 AFC Asian Cup]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://aawsat.com/node/5237374|title=روزنامة الموسم الكروي الجديد: 46 يوماً توقف من أجل «الآسيوية»... والجولة 27 تخنق الأندية|website=aawsat.com}}</ref>

The summer registration period for players is intended to start on 22 July and end on 6 September, meanwhile the winter one is set for 3 to 31 January 2027.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://arriyadiyah.com/892614/%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%86-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-22-%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88..-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-3-%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%B1|title=صيفية روشن تنطلق 22 يوليو.. والشتوية 3 يناير|website=arriyadiyah.com}}</ref>

== Teams ==
Consistent with the format established in the [[2023–24 Saudi Pro League|2023–24 season]], the competition features 18 teams. The clubs promoted from the First Division League include [[Abha Club|Abha]], returning after a two-season absence,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.voiceofemirates.com/en/sport/2026/04/21/officially-abha-is-the-first-team-to-qualify-for-the-saudi-roshan-league/|title=Officially.. Abha is the first team to qualify for the Saudi Roshan League|first=Ahmed|last=Khairy|date=21 April 2026}}</ref> [[Al Faisaly FC|Al-Faisaly]], returning after four seasons,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://koorabreak.com/en/291942/|title=Al-Faisaly seal return to Saudi Pro League after four-year absence – كورة بريك|date=14 May 2026}}</ref> and [[Diriyah Club|Al-Diriyah]], who secured their debut appearance by winning the promotion play-offs. Conversely, the relegated clubs consist of [[Al-Najma SC (Saudi Arabia)|Al-Najma]], following a one-season stint in the Premier League, [[Al-Okhdood Club|Al-Okhdood]], after three seasons, and [[Damac Club|Damac]], who return to the second tier to conclude a seven-season stay.

==Foreign players==
The amount of foreign players a club could register is ten, with eight players of any age and two players required to be aged 21 or younger. For league matches, clubs could only designate eight players as available for every match throughout the season but could designate ten as available for the [[King's Cup (Saudi Arabia)|King's Cup]] and [[Saudi Super Cup|Super Cup]].<ref>{{cite web |title=الاتحاد السعودي يعلن تنظيمات تعديل عدد اللاعبين لأندية دوري روشن السعودي |url=https://www.saff.com.sa/news.php?id=2729}}</ref>

*Players from other countries who were born in Saudi Arabia are counted as homegrown/local players.
