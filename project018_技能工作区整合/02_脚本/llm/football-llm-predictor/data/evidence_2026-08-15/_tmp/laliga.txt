The '''2026–27 La Liga''', also known as '''La Liga [[EA Sports]]'''<ref>{{cite news |title=EA SPORTS & LaLiga Announce Expansive New Partnership With EA SPORTS FC as Title Sponsor of All LaLiga Competitions |url=https://news.ea.com/press-releases/press-releases-details/2022/EA-SPORTS--LaLiga-Announce-Expansive-New-Partnership-With-EA-SPORTS-FC-as-Title-Sponsor-of-All-LaLiga-Competitions/default.aspx |publisher=Electronic Arts |date=2 August 2022 |access-date=27 April 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=EA SPORTS FC will be the title sponsor of all LaLiga competitions |url=https://www.laliga.com/en-GB/news/ea-sports-fc-will-be-the-title-sponsor-of-all-laliga-competitions|publisher=La Liga |date=2 August 2022 |access-date=14 May 2023}}</ref> due to sponsorship reasons, will be the 96th season of La Liga, Spain's top-flight football competition.
[[FC Barcelona|Barcelona]] are the two-time defending champions, having won their 29th title the previous season.

==Teams==
===Promotion and relegation (pre-season)===
A total of twenty teams contest the league, including seventeen sides from the [[2025–26 La Liga|2025–26 season]] and three promoted from the [[2025–26 Segunda División]]. This includes the two top teams from the Segunda División, and the winners of the [[2026 Segunda División play-offs|promotion play-offs]].

;Teams relegated to Segunda División
The first team to be relegated from La Liga was [[Real Oviedo|Oviedo]], after [[Girona FC|Girona]] earned a point in a 1–1 draw against [[Rayo Vallecano]] on 11 May 2026, after only a single season in the top tier.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-05-12 |title=Real Oviedo first team relegated after Rayo Vallecano 1-1 Girona draw |url=https://onefootball.com/en/news/real-oviedo-first-team-relegated-after-rayo-vallecano-1-1-girona-draw-42857598 |access-date=2026-05-13 |website=OneFootball |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Girona Draw With Rayo Vallecano as Real Oviedo Suffer La Liga Relegation |url=https://dailypioneer.com/news/real-oviedo-relegated-from-la-liga |access-date=2026-05-13 |website=dailypioneer.com |language=en}}</ref> The second and third team to be relegated were [[Girona FC|Girona]] and [[RCD Mallorca|Mallorca]] on 23 May 2026, after four and five years in top division respectively. 
;Teams promoted from Segunda División
On 16 May 2026, [[Racing de Santander|Racing Santander]] became the first side to mathematically be promoted, assured of a return to the top flight after a 14-year absence, following a 4–1 win against [[Real Valladolid]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/racing-santander-back-top-flight-183300159.html |title=Racing de Santander return to the top flight 14 years later |publisher=Yahoo! Sports |date=16 May 2026 }}</ref> [[Deportivo de A Coruña|Deportivo A Coruña]] became the second team to be promoted on the penultimate day after defeating Real Valladolid 2–0.<ref>{{cite web |last=Azzoni |first=Tales |url=https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/deportivo-la-coruna-returns-top-221617308.html |title=Deportivo La Coruna returns to top tier in Spain after eight years |publisher=Yahoo! Sports |date=25 May 2026 }}</ref>  [[Málaga CF|Málaga]], absent for a similar duration from La Liga, was the final promoted team by defeating [[UD Almería|Almería]] 2–1 in the promotion play-offs.
