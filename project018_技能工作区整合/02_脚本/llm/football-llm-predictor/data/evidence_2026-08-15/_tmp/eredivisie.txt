The '''2026–27 Eredivisie''', also known as '''VriendenLoterij Eredivisie''' for sponsorship reasons, will be the 71st season of [[Eredivisie]], the premier football competition in the [[Netherlands]]. The league games are scheduled to be held between 7 August 2026 and 23 May 2027.<ref>https://x.com/EredivisieEN/status/2002016065635709335</ref> [[PSV Eindhoven]] are the three-time defending champions.
