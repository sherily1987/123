The 2025–26 '''Saudi Pro League''' (known as the '''[[Roshn]] Saudi League''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 69th season of the [[Saudi Pro League|top-tier Saudi football league]], established in 1957, and the 18th season since it was rebranded as the '''Saudi Pro League''' in [[2008–09 Saudi Pro League|2008]].

[[Al-Ittihad Club (Jeddah)|Al-Ittihad]] were the defending champions, having won their 14th title [[2024–25 Saudi Pro League|last season]], however they failed to defend the league title this season, losing out to [[Al-Nassr FC|Al-Nassr]], who won their 11th league title. <ref>{{Cite web|url=https://spa.gov.sa/|title=رياضي / الاتحاد بطل لدوري المحترفين السعودي 2024 -2025|website=spa.gov.sa}}</ref> [[Al-Najma SC (Saudi Arabia)|Al-Najma]] and [[Neom SC|Neom]] earned automatic promotion to the Pro League, while [[Al-Hazem FC|Al-Hazem]] earned the last promotion spot through the play-offs. [[Al Raed S.FC|Al-Raed]], [[Al-Orobah FC|Al-Orobah]], and [[Al Wehda FC|Al-Wehda]] were relegated to the [[2025–26 Saudi First Division League]].

18 teams will compete in the league&nbsp;– the top 15 teams from the previous season and the 3 teams promoted from the [[2024–25 Saudi First Division League|FD League]].

===Teams who were promoted to the Pro League===

On 22 April 2025, [[Neom SC|Neom]] became the first team to be promoted following a 3–0 away win against [[Al-Arabi SC (Saudi Arabia)|Al-Arabi]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.kooora.com/%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85/%D8%A3%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%85-%D9%8A%D8%B5%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AE%D9%87/1403882|title=رسميًا.. نيوم يصعد إلى دوري روشن لأول مرة في تاريخه &#124; كووورة|website=www.kooora.com}}</ref> They were crowned champions following a 6–0 away win against [[Jeddah Club|Jeddah]] on 29 April 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.al-madina.com/article/932580/%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B6%D8%A9/%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%85-%D9%8A%D8%AD%D8%B3%D9%85-%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D9%84%D9%88|title=رسميًا.. نيوم يحسم دوري يلو|first=المدينة-|last=جدة|date=30 April 2025|website=جريدة المدينة}}</ref> Neom will play in the top flight of Saudi football for the first time in their history and will be the 40th side to participate in the Saudi Pro League since its inception. In addition, they will become the second side to represent the [[Tabuk Province]] following [[Al-Watani Club|Al-Watani]]'s promotion in 2007.

On 12 May 2025, [[Al-Najma SC (Saudi Arabia)|Al-Najma]] became the second club to be promoted following a 2–0 home win against [[Ohod Club|Ohod]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://arriyadiyah.com/866139|title=المرة الأولى.. النجمة يصعد إلى دوري المحترفين|website=arriyadiyah.com}}</ref> Al-Najma return to the top flight for the first time since getting relegated in the [[2002–03 Saudi Premier League|2002–03 season]] and will play in their 12th season in the top flight. In addition, this will be their first season in the Pro League era.

On 29 May 2025, [[Al-Hazem FC|Al-Hazem]] became the third and final club to be promoted after defeating [[Al-Adalah FC|Al-Adalah]] 3–0 in the promotion play-offs final.<ref>{{cite web |title=بعد فوزه على العدالة في الملحق.. الحزم يحجز آخر مقاعد الصعود إلى دوري المحترفين |url=https://spa.gov.sa/N2330574}}</ref> This was Al-Hazem's fourth promotion in seven years and will play in their 11th season in the top flight.

===Teams who were relegated to the FD League===

[[Al Raed S.FC|Al-Raed]] were the first team to be relegated following a 1–0 defeat away to [[Damac FC|Damac]] on 11 May 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://spa.gov.sa/|title=رياضي / الرائد يهبط لدوري الدرجة الأولى|website=spa.gov.sa}}</ref> Al-Raed were relegated after 17 consecutive seasons in the flight. This was also their first-ever relegation in the Pro League era.

On 25 May 2025, [[Al-Orobah FC|Al-Orobah]] became the second team to be relegated after they were docked three points for fielding an ineligible player against [[Al-Nassr FC|Al-Nassr]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.alarabiya.net/sport/2025/05/26/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B2%D8%B9-%D9%86%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%85|title=النصر ينتزع نقاط العروبة بقرار مركز التحكيم|date=26 May 2025|website=العربية}}</ref> Al-Orobah were relegated after one season in the top flight.

[[Al Wehda FC|Al-Wehda]] were the third and final club to be relegated, following a 2–1 away defeat to [[Al-Ettifaq Club|Al-Ettifaq]] on the final matchday.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://arriyadiyah.com/867044|title=الأخدود ينجو بريمونتادا مثيرة.. والوحدة يهبط|website=arriyadiyah.com}}</ref> Al-Wehda were relegated after three seasons in the top flight. In addition, this was their eighth relegation in history.
