The '''2025–26 Liga Portugal 2''' (also known as '''Liga Portugal 2 Meu Super''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 36th season of Portuguese football's second-tier league and the sixth season under the current [[Liga Portugal 2]] title.

== Overview ==
=== Promotion and relegation (pre-season) ===
18 teams are competing in this division, including three reserve sides from top-flight [[Primeira Liga]] teams – fifteen teams from previous season, one team relegated from [[2024–25 Primeira Liga|Primeira Liga]] and two teams promoted from [[2024–25 Liga 3 (Portugal)|Liga 3]].

==== Teams promoted to Primeira Liga ====
[[C.D. Tondela|Tondela]] and [[F.C. Alverca|Alverca]] secure promotion to Primeira Liga following the results of their respective matches on 17 May 2025. Tondela beat [[U.D. Leiria|União de Leiria]] and Alverca beat [[Portimonense S.C.|Portimonense]]. Tondela promoted after three seasons in second division while Alverca successfully achieved a second consecutive promotion after one season in the second division.<ref>{{cite web|last=|first=|date=17 May 2025|url=https://ligaportugal.pt/news/24764/cd-tondela-lifts-the-liga-portugal-2-meu-super-trophy/ |title=CD Tondela lifts the Liga Portugal 2 Meu Super trophy |language=en |website=Liga Portugal |access-date=6 July 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Anjinho |first=Jorge |date=16 May 2025|url=https://abola.pt/futebol/noticias/liga-2-alverca-nao-falha-e-festeja-a-subida-21-anos-depois-2025051621363391698/ |title=Liga 2: Alverca não falha e festeja a subida 21 anos depois! |language=pt |website=A Bola |access-date=6 July 2025}}</ref>

==== Teams relegated from Primeira Liga ====
[[S.C. Farense|Farense]] were relegated on 17 May 2025 with suffered a defeat to [[C.D. Santa Clara|Santa Clara]] and return to second division after spending two seasons in top division.<ref>{{cite web|last=Rodriguez |first=Hugo |date=17 May 2025|url=https://sulinformacao.pt/en/2025/05/farense-despromovido-a-ii-liga-apos-derrota-com-o-santa-clara/ |title=Farense relegated to the II League after defeat to Santa Clara |language=en |website=Sul Informação |access-date=15 June 2025}}</ref>

==== Teams promoted from Liga 3 ====
[[Lusitânia F.C.|Lusitânia Lourosa]] became the first team to be promoted on 3 May 2025 by defeating [[S.U. 1º Dezembro|1º Dezembro]] in matchweek 12 of the promotion phase. The result saw them play in the second division for the first time in their history.<ref>{{cite web|last=Nunes |first=David |date=3 May 2025|url=https://abola.pt/futebol/noticias/lusitania-de-lourosa-garante-subida-a-liga-2-2025050312035421792/ |title=Lusitânia de Lourosa garante subida à Liga 2 |language=pt |website=A Bola |access-date=4 July 2025}}</ref>

[[Sporting CP B]] became the second team to be promoted on 17 May 2025 by defeating [[Amarante F.C.|Amarante]] in matchweek 14 of the promotion phase. The result saw them return to the second division after a seven-season absence.<ref>{{cite web|last=Rodrigues |first=David Félix |date=17 May 2025|url=https://zerozero.pt/noticias/o-rugido-dos-i-leoezinhos-i-sete-anos-depois-sporting-b-regressa-a-ii-liga/817499/ |title=O rugido dos leõezinhos: sete anos depois, Sporting B regressa à II Liga |language=pt |website=ZeroZero |access-date=4 July 2025}}</ref>

==== Teams relegated to Liga 3 ====
[[C.D. Mafra|Mafra]] were relegated on 3 May 2025 after suffering a defeat to [[Leixões S.C.|Leixões]] and the result ended their time in the second division for five seasons.<ref>{{cite web|last=|first=|date=4 May 2025|url=https://zerozero.pt/noticias/cd-mafra-despromovido-a-liga-3-leixoes-agarra-manutencao/805478/ |title=CD Mafra despromovido à Liga 3, Leixões agarra manutenção |language=pt |website=ZeroZero |access-date=6 July 2025}}</ref>

=== Result from play-off ===
[[F.C. Vizela|Vizela]] failed to gain promotion to the Primeira Liga and remained in the second division for the season after losing 2-5 on aggregate to [[AVS Futebol SAD|AVS]] and [[F.C. Paços de Ferreira|Paços de Ferreira]] managed to retain their place in the second division after beating [[C.F. Os Belenenses|Belenenses]] 3-2 on aggregate in a two-legged promotion and relegation play-off.<ref>{{cite web|last=|first=|date=2 June 2025|url=https://ligaportugal.pt/news/24831/afs-draws-and-stays-in-the-liga-portugal-betclic/ |title=AFS draws and stays in the Liga Portugal Betclic |language=en |website=Liga Portugal |access-date=5 July 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=|first=|date=2 June 2025|url=https://ligaportugal.pt/news/24829/fc-p.ferreira-wins-in-extra-time-and-secures-promotion/ |title=FC P.Ferreira wins in extra time and secures promotion |language=en |website=Liga Portugal |access-date=6 July 2025}}</ref>

=== Reprived team ===
[[U.D. Oliveirense|Oliveirense]], who finished 17th last season and should have been relegated managed to secure a place in the second division as a result of [[Boavista F.C.|Boavista]] being demoted to the fifth division due to failing to register by the deadline set by the [[Liga Portuguesa de Futebol Profissional|league operator]].<ref>{{cite web|last=|first=|date=26 June 2025|url=https://bomdia.eu/derrota-financeira-atira-boavista-para-a-liga-3/ |title=Derrota financeira atira Boavista para a Liga 3 |language=pt |website=Bomdia |access-date=4 July 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Fernandes |first=Rafael |date=3 July 2025|url=https://abola.pt/futebol/noticias/oficial-candidatura-admitida-e-oliveirense-vai-jogar-na-liga-2-2025070316443114581/ |title=Oficial: candidatura admitida e Oliveirense vai jogar na Liga 2 |language=pt |website=A Bola |access-date=5 July 2025}}</ref>
